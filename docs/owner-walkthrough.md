# Walking the owners through Tegen HQ (ticket 18)

One meeting with Fredrik and Timmy, about an hour, before go-live. The goal: they can run a normal day in Tegen HQ on their own, they have made the settings that are theirs to make, and a go-live date is agreed. Show it on their own phones as much as on the big screen: most of the work happens on a phone between classes.

The Handbok category "Tegen HQ" has a guide for each part below; point to it at the end of each part instead of repeating everything.

## Before the meeting (Max)

- [ ] Fredrik and Timmy each have an account (Inställningar → Användare) and have signed in once. Production has two owner accounts, and only one has signed in so far (checked 2026-10-27).
- [ ] Last night's Mana sync ran (Inställningar → Koppling till Mana), so Medlemmar and the numbers on Home are fresh.
- [ ] Pick two or three real open leads and members to show; no made-up data in production.
- [ ] Bring the open questions at the end of this page.

## 1. Why, in two minutes

Every lead and every member who needs a word, in one place, on the phone. From go-live, Pipedrive and Notion are no longer where leads are worked: Tegen HQ is. The website keeps sending to email, Discord and the rest as today.

## 2. Sign in on the phone (5 min)

- Open the site, sign in, add it to the home screen.
- The bar at the bottom: Hem, Leads, Medlemmar, Mer (Handbok, Rapport, Timmar, Inställningar).

## 3. A day with leads (15 min)

Follow one real lead from Home. Guides: "Börja dagen i Tegen HQ", "Svara på en ny lead", "Stegen för en lead", "Provträningen", "Uppgifter och Vänta till".

- **Home:** Behöver din uppmärksamhet (deadlines first), Utan nästa steg, the Medlemmar card. Alla or Mina.
- **A lead:** Sammanfatta, so nobody reads the whole history. Ring. Under Vad hände? write a short note and press what happened. Or write it freely and press Föreslå steg, tick what is right, Spara valda.
- **Flytta till:** the next step is one button. Kall lead always asks why.
- **Provträning:** the date goes in, and the day after, a task to ask how it went appears by itself.
- **Uppgifter and Vänta till:** a lead with a task is taken care of until that day.
- **Ny lead** for someone who walks in or calls: also for a day-pass or klippkort person who wants to talk membership.

## 4. Medlemmar (10 min)

Guides: "Medlemmar: hör av dig till", "Från Mana", "Milstolpar".

- The groups: Säger upp, Fryser, Har slutat, Nya medlemmar, Aldrig incheckade, Slutade komma, Dagpass, Klippkort, Milstolpar. All of it comes from Mana each night; nobody adds members by hand.
- On a row: Ring, E-post (with Föreslå mejl when the assistant is on), Logga kontakt, Avfärda with a reason, Historik.
- Fördela shares the rows nobody has taken. Agree who takes what.
- Milstolpar: E-post with the owners' message filled in, or Klar if congratulated in person.

## 5. Handbok, Rapport, Timmar (8 min)

- **Handbok:** Tegen's routines. Who may write (Inställningar → Handbok), archive versus remove, the history of each routine.
- **Rapport:** leads per month and source, conversion, first-contact times. Fråga assistenten for anything else ("hur många provträningar i oktober?"). The weekly summary comes to Discord on Mondays.
- **Timmar:** Hämta från Mana, tick the categories, compare, Lås månaden, export for the accountant.

## 6. Settings that are theirs (15 min)

Make these together in the meeting; each is a few clicks.

- [ ] **Tidsgränser och helger:** the first-contact promise (24 hours) and when a lead counts as standing still in each stage.
- [ ] **Steg, anledningar och fält:** the stages and the Cold reasons. Also show the reasons Avfärda offers on Medlemmar (Skada, Sjukdom, Flyttat, Hittat ett annat gym, Pris, Ingen tid, Annat): these are not editable in the app yet, so note any change for Max.
- [ ] **E-post:** Medlemmar's templates for Aldrig incheckade and Slutade komma. Both are empty today; without them, E-post on a row starts blank.
- [ ] **Milstolpar:** the seven milestones and their messages: keep, change or turn off.
- [ ] **Koppling till Mana → Klippkort:** tick which Mana products are klippkort (none ticked today), or the Klippkort group stays empty.
- [ ] **Assistent:** the monthly cost cap (USD), and their own words that keep a note from ever being sent, on top of the built-in health words.
- [ ] **Användare:** accounts for any staff who will work leads or members.

## 7. Agree on go-live (5 min)

- [ ] The date. The day before, Max imports a fresh Pipedrive export so Home starts with today's leads (a replace refuses if anyone has worked imported leads in Tegen HQ, so no work is lost).
- [ ] From that day: new leads are worked only in Tegen HQ; Pipedrive and Notion are read-only.
- [ ] Notifications: which Discord channel the notices go to after the test channel.
- [ ] Who answers new leads within 24 hours, and on which days.
