# 04: Tasks and optional assignment

**What to build:** Owners create follow-up tasks with due dates on a lead and can optionally assign the lead to Fredrik or Timmy. A lead assigned to the other owner stays visible but greyed out.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A task has a title, due date and done state, and belongs to a lead
- [ ] Assigning is optional and defaults to unassigned
- [ ] Unassigned leads look normal for both owners
- [ ] A lead assigned to someone else is visible but greyed out for the other owner
- [ ] Overdue tasks are visibly marked
